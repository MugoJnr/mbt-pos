## 2. What was implemented

A cashier cannot type a past date on the sales screen. They open **Backdating Requests** on the till or the shop dashboard, pick a past business date, write a reason, and enter one or more sales. Saving a draft or submitting it does not create official sales, stock movements, receipts, or customer debts.

A manager, admin, or super-admin reviews the request. They can return it with a note, reject it with a reason, or approve it. Approval posts every sale in one database transaction through the existing stock and debt rules. The sale’s business date is the requested date. `created_at` stays the real posting time. Each official sale is linked to the request with `sales.backdate_request_id` and a unique `client_token` of `backdate-{request}-{sale}`.

Statuses: draft, submitted, returned, approved, rejected, withdrawn.

A cashier sees only their own requests. A viewer cannot open the queue. A cashier cannot approve. Approving twice returns the existing receipts and does not post again. A stale version returns 409. If stock is short, the whole request rolls back.

M-Pesa is recorded with the reference the cashier typed. Approval does not mark that reference as verified. Credit and part-payment create the debt only when the request is approved. A deposit is a payment, not extra sales revenue.

The request queue lives in the shop SQLite database. Approved sales are queued for sync the same way as other sales. There is no separate cloud request table.

## 4. Permissions and transitions

| Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Cashier | Create, edit their draft or a returned request, submit, withdraw a draft, see their own history. |
| Another cashier | Cannot open someone else’s request (404). |
| Viewer | 403. |
| Manager, admin, super-admin | Review, return, reject, approve. They can edit a submitted request. The edit is an event, not a silent overwrite. |
| Cashier | Cannot approve (403), including their own request. |

Allowed moves: draft to submitted or withdrawn; submitted to approved, rejected, returned, or withdrawn; returned to submitted after an edit. Approved and rejected requests stay as they are. A rejected request is not rewritten into an approval.

## 10. How to verify

1. Use a copy of a shop database, never the live shop file.
2. Sign in as a cashier. Open **Backdating Requests**. Choose yesterday, enter a reason, add at least one sale, and submit.
3. Confirm today’s sales, stock, and debts have not changed.
4. Sign in as a manager. Open the same request, read the totals, and approve.
5. Confirm the receipt uses the past business date, stock moved once, and approving again does not add another receipt.
6. To roll the program back, use the 3.1.69 installer in `C:\MBT_Build\release_3.1.69`. Leave any already posted sales in place.
